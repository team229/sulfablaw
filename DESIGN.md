## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the stately rigor, intellectual authority, and measured poise of a premier modern legal practice. Moving away from tired, heavy corporate dark navy, the aesthetic embraces an editorial, museum-grade balance: pristine gallery whites, deep imperial crimson accents, and warm architectural taupe neutrals derived from classical stone fluting.

The design philosophy combines **high-end editorial typography** with **disciplined corporate minimalism**. Visual density is generous and deliberate, evoking high-touch bespoke counsel rather than transactional software. High contrast between crisp parchment backdrops and deep crimson keystones conveys uncompromising precision, discernment, and sovereign institutional trust.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the classical interplay between rich crimson and architectural limestone:

- **Primary (`#8B1E3F` - Crimson Wine)**: The defining signature color. Used with disciplined restraint on focal interaction points, hero statements, active tabs, and primary legal CTAs. Conveys historic prestige and focused authority.
- **Secondary (`#A89F91` - Architectural Taupe)**: Derived from the ionic column capitals of the mark. Applied to fine dividing rules, structural framing, pill backgrounds, and subtle badges. Adds warmth and institutional permanence.
- **Tertiary (`#1E293B` - Slate Charcoal)**: The ink token for long-form case law, brief titles, and heavy typography. Softer and more sophisticated than jet black, retaining clarity across dense legal documents.
- **Neutral (`#FAFAFA` - Alabaster Parchment)**: The foundational backdrop across canvas zones, paired with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card containers and warm sand borders (`#E5E0D8`) to generate physical paper layering.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances classical jurisprudence with ultra-legible modern execution:

- **Display & Headlines (`EB Garamond`)**: Bestows an editorial, book-press character reminiscent of legal statutes, court rulings, and prestigious publications. Headings use optical kerning and measured leading to ensure gravitas.
- **Body & Data (`Manrope`)**: A clean, balanced sans-serif providing maximum legibility for complex contracts, fee arrangements, attorney biographies, and multi-column document indices.
- **Micro-Labels & Meta (`Manrope` Uppercase)**: Used across dates, practice area categories, and bar admission badges. Styled with wide tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) for quiet, authoritative taxonomy.

## Layout & Spacing

The framework relies on a 12-column structured grid designed to mimic the proportions of printed legal folios:

- **Canvas Margins**: Compact on mobile (`1.25rem`), expanding to an executive boundary (`4rem`) on desktop screens to provide expansive white space around critical briefings.
- **Rhythm & Grid Alignment**: Practice overviews and partner rosters break into a 3-column or 4-column balanced architecture, with a mandatory 2rem gutter on desktop to keep white document cards distinct.
- **Editorial Proportions**: Dense data tables alternate with breathable section margins (`space-xl`), ensuring the eye travels seamlessly without cognitive fatigue during long legal assessments.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons heavy synthetic dropshadows in favor of **fine architectural borders and soft ambient diffusion**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas)**: Backgrounds reside on `#FAFAFA`, providing a warm natural paper tone.
- **Tier 1 (Cards & Briefs)**: White surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) framed by hair-thin borders (`1px solid #E5E0D8`) and an imperceptible, feather-light ambient shadow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`).
- **Tier 2 (Modals, Overlays & Floating Headers)**: Floated panels retain a crisp border (`#A89F91` at 30% opacity) accompanied by an editorial drop shadow (`0 20px 40px -10px rgba(139, 30, 63, 0.08), 0 1px 3px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.05)`), adding rich burgundy warmth to the elevation.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 1 (Soft)** geometry (`0.25rem` / `4px` base radii), intentionally avoiding informal or playful high-rounded forms:

- **Cards & Data Modules**: Defined with crisp `4px` corners (`rounded-sm`), evoking physical bond paper, linen folders, and legal briefs.
- **Buttons & Control Elements**: Uniform `4px` corner radii reinforce disciplined, no-nonsense executive utility.
- **Pills & Status Badges**: Reserved strictly for high-level category pills (`rounded-full`), balancing structural rectangular boxes with subtle classical curvature.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid crimson (`#8B1E3F`) fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` radius, subtle transition to deep maroon (`#6E1630`) on hover. Height of `44px` with `Manrope` medium weight.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Clean white fill, framed in `1px solid #A89F91`, charcoal text (`#1E293B`). Crimson border on hover.
- **Tertiary / Text Link**: Slate charcoal text with a crimson border underline offset by `4px`, transitioning smoothly to full crimson on hover.

### Cards & Case Modules
- Constructed on pure `#FFFFFF` over `#FAFAFA` canvas.
- Perimeter wrapped in `1px solid #E5E0D8`.
- Optional top accent rule: `2px` solid `#8B1E3F` stripe reserved for high-stakes matter results or featured counsel.

### Chips & Practice Badges
- Uppercase micro-typography (`label-sm`).
- Background in subtle taupe wash (`#F4F1ED`), text in `#1E293B`, bordered with `#E5E0D8`. Active chips utilize crimson background with crisp white typography.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #D6D0C7` borders.
- Active focus state: perimeter tint in `#8B1E3F` with an unobtrusive `0 0 0 3px rgba(139, 30, 63, 0.12)` halo.
- Labels styled in `Manrope` medium slate charcoal floating crisply above the field.

### Lists & Legal Citations
- Dividers between matter items leverage hairline strokes of `#E5E0D8`.
- Left-aligned crimson dot or subtle taupe numbering for case indices and statutory lists.
