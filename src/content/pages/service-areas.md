## About Immigration Legal Services in the Bay Area

Immigration law is federal, yet outcomes depend on the forum. Immigration judges decide removal cases, USCIS handles most applications and petitions, and federal courts review whether detention is lawful and whether agencies acted within the law.

People in removal proceedings may qualify for asylum, withholding of removal, Convention Against Torture protection, cancellation of removal or adjustment of status, depending on their facts. Related problems include missed deadlines, prior removal orders, re-detention after release, denied bond hearings and long USCIS delays.
