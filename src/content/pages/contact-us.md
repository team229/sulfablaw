## Immigration Legal Services We Can Help With

When you contact us, you do not need to know exactly which legal category your case falls into. That is part of what we figure out together. Here are the main areas where people reach out.

### Deportation and Removal Defense

If you have received a Notice to Appear or have a hearing in immigration court, a **deportation defense lawyer** or **removal defense attorney** can review your history and explain whether you may qualify for relief such as asylum, cancellation of removal, adjustment of status, or a waiver. Do not wait for the hearing date to get advice.

### Asylum Applications

An **asylum lawyer** helps you prepare a case that shows a well-founded fear of persecution on account of race, religion, nationality, political opinion, or membership in a particular social group. A one-year filing deadline applies, with limited exceptions, so early advice matters. If you need an **immigration attorney for deportation and asylum** matters, bring every document you have, even those you think are minor.

### Immigration Detention and Bond Hearings

If someone you love is being held by ICE, our **immigration detention lawyer** work includes evaluating bond eligibility, preparing for bond hearings, and, where the government has not provided the process the law requires, pursuing relief in federal court.

### Federal Immigration Litigation and Habeas Corpus

A habeas corpus petition asks a federal judge to decide whether detention is lawful. Maisoun files these petitions and, in urgent situations, emergency motions for temporary restraining orders. If you are looking for an **immigration detention and federal litigation lawyer**, mention that when you contact us so we can move quickly.

### Family-Based Immigration and Green Cards

A **family-based immigration attorney** and **green card lawyer** reviews eligibility before filing, prepares petitions, and responds to USCIS requests for evidence. Small mistakes in these filings can cause large delays, so a short consultation before you file is often worthwhile.

### Immigration Appeals

If an immigration judge denied your case, the deadline to appeal is short, usually 30 days. Contact us right away so we can review the decision.

## If Someone Was Just Detained: Urgent Steps

If this is an emergency, call first and read the rest later. In the meantime, here is what usually helps most.

### Gather the Basics

- Full legal name, date of birth, and country of birth
- The A-number (Alien Registration Number), if you have it
- Where and when the arrest took place
- Any paperwork the person received, such as a Notice to Appear or bond notice
- Immigration history: how and when they entered, prior applications, earlier court dates

### Locate the Person

The ICE Online Detainee Locator can show where someone is being held. People are often moved quickly and can end up far from the Bay Area. If you find the location, give it to us when you call.

### Do Not Sign Anything Without Advice

Detained people are sometimes handed forms, including voluntary departure paperwork, and told to sign. Signing can end your options. Ask to speak with a lawyer first.

### Why Speed Matters

In detention cases, the first days can shape what is possible. A federal habeas petition and emergency motion can sometimes be filed quickly, but only if we have the facts. The public record shows that federal judges have, in some cases, ordered release or required bond hearings after such filings. Whether that applies in a particular situation depends on the person's history and the circumstances of the arrest, and no outcome is guaranteed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Service-Related Questions

#### What does an immigration lawyer do?

An immigration lawyer advises and represents people in matters such as removal defense, asylum, family petitions, green cards, visas, and detention. They prepare filings, gather evidence, appear before USCIS, immigration courts, and federal courts, and explain the risks of each option.

#### Can an immigration attorney help someone get out of detention?

Sometimes. Depending on the person's history and the facts of the arrest, options may include a bond hearing before an immigration judge or a habeas corpus petition in federal court. Eligibility varies and nothing is guaranteed, so a prompt review is the best way to learn what applies.

#### What is a habeas corpus petition in an immigration case?

It is a federal court filing that challenges the legality of someone's detention. Courts have used it to order bond hearings, require notice before re-detention, and in some cases order release.

#### Do I need a lawyer for a family-based green card?

No law requires it, but mistakes can cause delays, denials, or bigger problems if there is a prior overstay, unlawful presence, or criminal history. A short consultation before filing often prevents costly errors.

#### How long does an asylum case take?

It depends on whether the case is with USCIS or in immigration court, and on current backlogs. Some cases take months and others take years. After reviewing where your case stands, an attorney can give a more realistic estimate.

## About Immigration Law: What You Should Know

Immigration law governs who may enter, live, work, and remain in the United States. It is federal law, built on the Immigration and Nationality Act, agency regulations and policy, and court decisions. Because enforcement priorities and policies change, the same facts can lead to different results depending on timing.

The main areas include family-based immigration, employment and visa categories, asylum and humanitarian protection, removal defense, and challenges to unlawful detention. Cases can move through USCIS, immigration court, the Board of Immigration Appeals, and federal court, and each forum has its own deadlines. Missing one can close off options.

If you are not sure which category your case falls into, a consultation with an immigration lawyer is the fastest way to find out.
