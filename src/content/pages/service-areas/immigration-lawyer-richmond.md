## Immigration Law Firm Services for Richmond Clients

### Immigration Detention, Bond, and Habeas Corpus

If ICE has detained you or a loved one, the situation can change quickly. People can be transferred to facilities far from the Bay Area, sometimes within a day.

Maisoun focuses on federal litigation to challenge unlawful immigration detention. A habeas corpus petition asks a federal judge to decide whether the government has the legal authority to keep someone in custody. When the situation is urgent, we can file an emergency motion for a temporary restraining order to stop a transfer or secure release while the case continues. Common arguments center on due process rights, including the right to an individualized bond hearing in front of a neutral decision-maker and the right to notice before someone is re-detained.

If you are looking for an immigration detention lawyer or a bond hearing attorney in Richmond, call as soon as possible and have the detained person's full name and A-number available.

### Deportation and Removal Defense

A Notice to Appear starts a removal case. It does not mean the case is lost. As a removal defense attorney, Maisoun looks at the full picture, including how you came to the U.S., your family relationships, your time here, and your record, to determine whether relief such as asylum, cancellation of removal, adjustment of status, or a waiver may be available. Then we prepare the evidence and make the arguments to the immigration judge.

### Asylum Applications

Asylum is for people who have suffered persecution, or fear it, because of race, religion, nationality, political opinion, or membership in a particular social group. A successful case rests on a consistent account, solid supporting documents, and credible country-condition evidence. We help clients build each piece and get ready for interviews and hearings. Because of the one-year filing deadline and its limited exceptions, don't wait to ask about timing.

### Family-Based Immigration and Green Cards

Keeping families together is a goal for many Richmond clients. We handle family-based petitions for spouses, children, and parents, green card applications, and complications such as prior overstays or requests for evidence. A green card lawyer can often prevent problems that would otherwise surface months later.

### Immigration Appeals and Federal Court Review

If an immigration judge or USCIS decides against you, you may still have remedies: a motion to reopen or reconsider, an appeal to the Board of Immigration Appeals, or federal court review. We also file federal cases when an agency has unreasonably delayed or unlawfully withheld a decision. Deadlines for these steps are strict.

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### What does an immigration lawyer do?

An immigration lawyer advises on and handles green cards, family petitions, asylum, citizenship, deportation defense, and detention. That includes preparing filings, building evidence, representing you before USCIS and immigration judges, and going to federal court when needed.

#### What can be done if someone is detained by ICE?

Depending on the facts, options may include requesting a bond hearing, seeking release, or filing a habeas corpus petition in federal court. Results can't be guaranteed, but quick action matters.

#### How much does an immigration lawyer cost?

Costs depend on the type of case and the work required. A straightforward application costs less than a removal defense case or federal court litigation. We explain fees at the first consultation.

#### Can a lawyer speed up my case?

A lawyer can't control USCIS processing times, but can make sure the filing is correct and complete, respond to requests on time, and, if appropriate, go to federal court when an agency is unreasonably delaying a decision.

#### Do I need a lawyer to get a green card?

It isn't required, but it is wise if you have an overstay, an arrest, a past denial, or any question about eligibility.

#### Where are immigration court hearings for Richmond residents?

#### Do you meet with clients in Richmond?

#### My family member was moved to a detention center far from Richmond. Can you still help?

## About Immigration Law

Immigration law is the body of federal rules that decides who may enter, live, and work in the United States, and what happens when someone is accused of breaking those rules. Its main source is the Immigration and Nationality Act. USCIS, ICE, and CBP carry it out, immigration courts run by the Executive Office for Immigration Review hear cases, and decisions can be appealed to the Board of Immigration Appeals and reviewed in federal court.

Key areas include:

- **Family-based immigration:** Petitions by U.S. citizens and permanent residents for family members.
- **Asylum and related protection:** Relief for people who fear persecution or torture.
- **Removal defense:** Contesting deportation charges and applying for relief.
- **Detention and bond:** Limits on when and how long someone can be held, and when a bond hearing is required.
- **Appeals and federal litigation:** Review of agency and court decisions, including habeas corpus petitions.

Immigration policy shifts often, through memos, executive actions, and court decisions. Advice that was right a year ago may not be right now, so it helps to work with an attorney who keeps up with current developments.
